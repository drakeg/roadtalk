# RoadTalk public-beta terms — review draft

Status: **draft for product/legal review; not approved terms**

This file exists so the launch gate has concrete content to review. It is not legal advice, has not been reviewed by legal counsel, and must not be presented to users as final terms.

## Service scope

RoadTalk is a location-aware, voice-first communication service. Public-beta scope, supported devices, availability, and any paid/provider-backed capability must match the separately approved launch configuration.

## Safety and acceptable use

Users must use RoadTalk lawfully and safely. RoadTalk is not an emergency service, does not guarantee message/audio delivery, and must not be relied on as a substitute for emergency communications. Users remain responsible for complying with driving, radio/communications, privacy, and local laws.

Abuse, harassment, unauthorized access, interference with service, attempts to obtain another user's private location/data, and other harmful or unlawful use may result in restriction or termination.

## Accounts and content

Users are responsible for protecting account/recovery credentials. Public pseudonyms such as callsigns may be visible to other users only within approved product behavior. RoadTalk must not claim ownership of user content beyond rights actually required to operate the service.

## Availability and beta changes

A beta may contain defects, change, or be unavailable. Any limitation of liability, warranty disclaimer, dispute provision, age requirement, geographic restriction, termination right, governing law, and contact information requires explicit final review before publication.

## Paid features and third parties

No real-money payment, store purchase, premium provider, or paid external service is authorized by this draft. If such features are later approved, final terms must describe fees, renewal/cancellation/refund behavior, provider dependencies, and applicable store/payment rules.

## Privacy

Data handling is governed by the separately reviewed privacy notice. The terms must not make broader data-use or compliance claims than RoadTalk's verified implementation.

## Unresolved before publication

Final review must resolve legal entity/contact identity, eligibility/age, governing law, dispute terms, warranties/liability, IP/content license language, termination/deletion effects, app-store requirements, and any jurisdiction-specific obligations.
